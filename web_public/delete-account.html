Delete your account inside the app Fastest

1
Open Ulama Circle and go to the Profile tab.
2
Scroll down and tap Delete account.
3
Confirm on the prompt. Your account and data are removed immediately and permanently.

Guests (users who never signed in) have no account to delete — simply uninstall the app.

Prefer to request it by email?

Email us from the address linked to your account with the subject “Delete my account”, and we'll erase it within 30 days.

omomummyalaba@gmail.com

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign‑in (Google / email)
  • Your profile (name, avatar, language preferences)
  • Your favorites, playlists, and listening history

What we keep

  • Nothing that personally identifies you.
  • Anonymous, aggregated usage counts (e.g. total plays of a lecture) that cannot be traced back to you may remain.

Downloaded audio saved on your device is removed when you delete your account or uninstall the app.
